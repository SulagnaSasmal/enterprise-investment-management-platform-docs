About Asset Atrium Manager

SunBridge Asset Atrium Manager (now part of FGS) is an enterprise investment management platform supporting the complete trade lifecycle, portfolio management, fund accounting, and regulatory reporting. Deployed on-premise or managed hosting, it serves asset managers, hedge funds, insurance companies, pension funds, and fund administrators across 30+ countries.

Core modules

Asset Atrium Investment Manager (ATIM)

  • Portfolio hierarchy (funds, sub-funds, strategies, sleeves)
  • Order management with pre-trade compliance
  • FIX 4.2/4.4 order routing to broker venues
  • Block/allocation workflows with pro-rata models
  • Position keeping with real-time P&L
  • Corporate actions processing
  • Performance attribution (Brinson-Fachler, GIPS)

Asset Atrium Settlement Manager

  • SWIFT MT5xx settlement instruction generation
  • Custodian reconciliation (position, cash, transaction)
  • Failed trade management and aging dashboards
  • SSI master and counterparty routing
  • CLS and T2S settlement connectivity

Asset Atrium Fund Administrator

  • NAV calculation (official, shadow, indicative)
  • Multi-GAAP fund accounting (IFRS, US GAAP)
  • Investor servicing: subscriptions, redemptions, transfers
  • Fee calculation: management, performance (HWM, hurdle)
  • Regulatory reporting: UCITS, AIFMD, Form PF

Cross-module services

  • Instrument master: equities, FI, derivatives, FX, alternatives
  • Market data: Bloomberg B-PIPE, BVAL, Refinitiv, ICE
  • Compliance: pre-trade, post-trade, mandate rules
  • Reporting: Crystal Reports, SSRS, Excel-based
  • Audit trail: full change history across all entities

Processing models

  • Online: interactive order entry, portfolio inquiries via ATIM thick client or web UI
  • Event-driven: JMS-based async processing for trade events, corporate actions, downstream updates
  • Batch: NAV calculation, EOD pricing, fund accounting close, historization, regulatory reports

Operational requirements

  • 24×7 uptime with controlled business date rollover
  • Multi-market calendar support (60+ exchanges, 40+ clearing houses)
  • Idempotent batch reruns and full audit traceability
  • WebLogic clustering and Oracle RAC for high availability
  • DR with RPO <15 min, RTO <4 hours for critical functions
