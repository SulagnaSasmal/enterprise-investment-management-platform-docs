Enterprise Investment Management Platform

Multi-time zone processing
Zone segmentation, calendars, and trade-date logic

Problem Statement

Global investment operations must process portfolios across different market calendars and time zones. “End of day” is not a single moment worldwide; batch operations must align to each portfolio’s zone.

Core Concepts

  • Processing zone: a named zone aligned to a market calendar (e.g., region or country grouping).
  • Portfolio-to-zone association: each portfolio is assigned a zone that defines trading day boundaries.
  • Zone-based batch segmentation: batch jobs execute per zone, with zone-specific valuation dates.
  • Trade date validation: a trade’s effective date must match the portfolio’s zone calendar rules.

D+1 Trade Enablement (Conceptual)

In some operating models, a platform may accept “D+1” trades depending on market rules. The key is that acceptance is deterministic and auditable per portfolio zone.

  • Validate trade date against zone calendar (holidays, weekends, market closures).
  • Ensure downstream processing (allocation, confirmation, settlement) uses a consistent business date.
  • Record the rule decision and calendar version in audit trails.

Database Impact (Abstracted)

Typical data changes include adding a zone reference to portfolios and storing processing boundaries in configuration. Exact schemas are intentionally not provided.

The differentiator here is not “knowing time zones”; it’s documenting how time zones, calendars, and batch windows intersect with the trading lifecycle.
